I am an incoming MSc student in [Embedded Systems Engineering](https://www.tf.uni-freiburg.de/en/study-programs/embedded-systems-engineering/m-sc-embedded-systems-engineering) at the [**University of Freiburg**](https://uni-freiburg.de/en/), where I study at the intersection of computer science and microsystems engineering. I hold a BTech in Electronics and Computer Engineering from [Mahindra University](https://www.mahindrauniversity.edu.in/), and spent an exchange semester at [Centrale Lille](https://centralelille.fr/) in France.

<!-- Change this -->

My interests lie in **embedded machine learning** and the **reliability of computing hardware** — bringing neural networks onto resource-constrained microcontrollers, and understanding how they behave when the hardware beneath them is imperfect. Most recently, through a research internship at [École Centrale de Lyon](https://www.ec-lyon.fr/en), I have been working on **statistical fault injection** for quantized convolutional neural networks running on Arm Cortex-M microcontrollers, studying how bit-level faults in registers propagate into a model's predictions.

<!-- Change this -->

I care about the full stack of an embedded system, from the silicon and instruction set up to the model running on it. Looking ahead, I hope to deepen this work across **edge AI, cyber-physical systems, and dependable circuit design** — themes I am excited to explore further at Freiburg.

I am always glad to connect with fellow students, researchers, and anyone working on robotics or embedded systems. Please feel free to reach out.
